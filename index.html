How We Work

Find the Cause, Not Just the Symptom

Every roof has a different story. These are the habits behind every Roof Worth visit, from the first phone call to the final repair.

Roof Inspections

A Hands-On Look, Not Just a Drone

Your inspection starts before we arrive: your address lets us study the roof on satellite imagery and plan the visit. On the roof, we check what a photo from the air can’t: loose tiles, ridge mortar, flashing and the small cracks behind a leak.

  • Your roof studied before the visit
  • Tiles, flashing and mortar checked by hand
  • Photos of every area of concern

Tile Roofs · Underlayment

What’s Under Your Tile Matters

Cracked tiles are only part of the story. When it’s needed, tile comes off so the underlayment and battens can be inspected and the real problem fixed, not just covered up.

  • Underlayment and battens inspected
  • Cracked and loose tiles repaired
  • Vent pipes checked for hidden attic leaks

Repair · Don’t Replace

A Repair When a Repair Is Enough

An older roof is not automatically a failed roof. You get a clear explanation of what is wrong, a detailed scope of work, and an honest answer about what doesn’t need fixing yet.

  • The problem explained in plain words
  • A detailed scope of work
  • Straight answers on what can wait
